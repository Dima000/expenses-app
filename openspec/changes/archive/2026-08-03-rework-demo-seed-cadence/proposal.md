## Why

The demo seed generator places entries with a single global `dayIndex` driving
date, category, *and* amount through small moduli. Three consequences, all
visible in the shipped demo:

1. **The current month collapses.** `maxDay = today.getDate()` combined with
   `day = 1 + (slot % maxDay)` puts all ~30 current-month entries on 1–2 days
   early in a month. On 2026-08-02 every August entry lands on Aug 1 or Aug 2.
   The demo therefore looks broken near the start of every month and recovers
   near the end — a shared link that degrades on a monthly cycle.
2. **Per-category density is far too low for the drilldown heatmap.** The
   heatmap renders one category's daily totals for one month. Round-robining
   eight categories at one entry per day leaves any single category with 1–3
   lit cells out of ~30. `groceries` in July 2026 — a fully healthy month —
   lights **two** cells.
3. **The data reads as generated.** Exactly one entry every day, never zero and
   never two; amounts are `min + dayIndex % range`, so heatmap intensity ramps
   linearly and wraps instead of varying.

Part 4 of the demo-mode work (`docs/explorations/2026-08-01-demo-mode-and-dev-sandbox.md`)
is where marketing screenshots get taken. They are not worth taking against the
current dataset, so this lands first.

## What Changes

- **Replace the global-`dayIndex` cycler with per-category cadence rules.**
  Each category declares a rhythm — `weekly(weekdays, skipEvery?)` or
  `monthly(dayOfMonth)` — and entries are generated by walking the covered days
  and asking each category whether it fires. Placement becomes a function of
  `(category, date)` rather than of a running counter, so changing one
  category's cadence no longer reshuffles every other entry in the dataset.
- **At least two categories get heatmap-grade density.** `groceries` (Tue+Sat)
  and `relationships` (Fri+Sat) land on roughly 7–9 distinct days per full
  month. `utilities` stays deliberately at one day per month so drilldowns
  differ from one another instead of being uniformly sparse.
- **Hand-picked amount cycles replace `min + dayIndex % range`.** Each category
  carries a short list of authored amounts cycled by its own occurrence index.
  Deterministic, readable, and free of the linear intensity ramp. **No PRNG is
  introduced** — the generator remains a pure function of "today".
- **Widen the coverage window** from "3–4 months of the current year + 3 months
  of the prior year" to **the full prior calendar year plus the current year to
  date** (20 months and ~690 rows on 2026-08-02, measured against a prototype of
  the cadence table in design.md). Both the prior-year and
  current-year Reports year-views are then populated rather than showing 3–4
  bars against nine blank months.
- **Partial months fall out of the cadence walk.** Generation stops at "today",
  so the current month is naturally proportional instead of carrying a full
  month's volume compressed onto the elapsed days. The `maxDay` wrap is deleted
  rather than patched.
- **Test assertions shift from an exact total to intent-expressing bands.**
  `expect(rows.length).toBe(210)` is no longer derivable once volume is
  emergent; it is replaced by a per-month volume band plus a new assertion that
  at least two categories reach ≥8 distinct days in a full month.

Not changing: determinism (same "today" ⇒ identical output), the absence of
randomness, `source: 'web'` on every row, the required mix (uncategorized,
`needsReview`, an entry dated today), local-only demo categories, and
`applyAutoCategory` producing the auto-matched entries.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `demo-data-source`: the "Seed data covers the required date range, volume,
  and mix" requirement is rewritten — the coverage window becomes the full
  prior calendar year plus the current year to date, fixed per-month volume is
  replaced by per-category cadences, no entry may be dated after "today", and a
  new per-category density guarantee is added so the drilldown heatmap is
  populated for at least two categories.

## Impact

- `web/src/lib/demoSeedData.ts` — the placement, category-selection, and amount
  logic is replaced. `buildDemoCategories`, `DEMO_OWNER_UID`, and the exported
  signature of `generateDemoSpendings(today, categories)` are unchanged, so
  `DemoDataSource` needs no edit.
- `web/src/lib/demoSeedData.test.ts` — exact-count assertion replaced by bands;
  new density and no-future-dates assertions added.
- `openspec/specs/demo-data-source/spec.md` — via the delta spec.
- Row count grows from 210 to roughly 690 in-memory objects; no measurable cost
  for an in-memory array, and no change to `DemoDataSource`'s subscription
  behavior.
- No production/Firestore path is touched — the generator is demo-only.
